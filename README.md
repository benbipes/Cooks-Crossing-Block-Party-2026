# 🎈 Cooks Crossing Annual Block Party & Pig Pickin' 2026

A modern, mobile-first neighborhood potluck coordination website designed specifically for the **Cooks Crossing Annual Block Party & Pig Pickin'**.

Hosted at the field near Julie Byers' home at the corner of **Wiembley and Brentford** on **Saturday, October 3rd, 2026 at 4:00 PM**.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **📸 Custom Hero Background**: Features the neighborhood hot air balloon landing photo with an atmospheric gradient overlay for crisp readability.
- **🐷 James Strickland's Pig Pickin'**: Highlights James cooking a whole pig for everyone at 5:00 PM.
- **🥗 Interactive Potluck Side Dish Tracker**:
  - Homeowners enter their **Name** and **Dish Name** (with optional Category, Dietary notes, and special instructions).
  - **Persistent storage**: Entries are automatically saved to `localStorage` and never lost on page reload.
  - Users are not permitted to delete entries.
  - Interactive search bar, category filters, and dietary filter toggles.
  - "😋 Can't wait!" cheer reaction counter for each dish.
  - Confetti celebration upon signing up!
- **🎯 Friendly Lawn Games**: Mentions friendly open play for cornhole, volleyball, and yard games.
- **📱 Mobile-First Priority**: Persistent bottom navigation dock, mobile segmented view control (`What's Added` vs `Add My Dish`), 16px touch inputs preventing iOS auto-zoom, and safe area insets.
- **⏱️ Live Event Countdown**: Real-time countdown to Saturday, October 3rd, 2026 at 4:00 PM.
- **🖨️ Day-of Checklist & Print Mode**: Optimized print stylesheet (`@media print`) so organizers can print a clean checklist for the food table.
- **📥 CSV Export**: One-click download of the complete potluck list for spreadsheets.
- **☁️ Multi-Household Sync Options**:
  - **Shareable Snapshot Link**: Generates a link with current potluck dishes encoded for sharing via text or email.
  - **Optional Firebase Live Sync**: Free, serverless real-time database integration across multiple devices.
