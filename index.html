Party Day Details & FAQ

Everything you need to know about the Cooks Crossing Block Party

Where exactly is the party located?

The event will be hosted at the field adjacent to Julie Byers' home at the corner of Wiembley and Brentford. Tables, shade canopies, games, and the pig roast smoker will be set up there!

What is provided vs. what should we bring?

Provided for you: Whole roasted pig & pulled pork (thanks to James Strickland!), barbecue buns & sauces, paper plates, napkins, forks, water dispensers, and ice.

Please bring: Your potluck side dish or dessert (labeled!), lawn chairs or picnic blankets, and personal beverages or coolers.

Can I bring hot dishes or crockpots?

Yes! Julie has outdoor power strips and heavy-duty extension cords set up at the warming table for slow cookers and crockpots. Please mark "Needs Outlet" on your dish signup so we allocate enough plugs!

Where should we park?

Walking or golf-carting over is highly encouraged! If driving, please park along one side of Brentford so emergency vehicles and through traffic can easily pass.
